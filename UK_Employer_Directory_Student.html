How to use. Each file is a complete, self-contained employer search for one nation or region: search from any postcode with a distance radius, filter by company name, sector, organisation type and advanced filters (status, size, council area, address status), see the results on the map and in the table, open organisation profiles and export to Excel. Postcode search works within a file's own region; this landing page is the switchboard. The directory shows organisations, not advertised vacancies, so treat every result as a lead to research and approach. No contact details exist in any source and none are guessed.

Sources (all Open Government Licence v3): Companies House free bulk data; OS Code-Point Open; Charity Commission for England and Wales; OSCR; NHS ODS and Public Health Scotland; Get Information About Schools; mySociety local-authority dataset.
Known gaps: Northern Ireland charities and health trusts, three Welsh NHS trusts, UK central government departments, NI postcode coordinates (not openly licensed). Councils outside Scotland are placed at an authority reference point rather than a verified HQ postcode.
